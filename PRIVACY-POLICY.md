# BlockMate Privacy Policy

**Effective date: November 4, 2026**

BlockMate is a companion app for Minecraft Bedrock survival worlds. This policy explains what the app accesses and how it is used.

## Information BlockMate accesses

When you choose a JSON building blueprint, BlockMate reads that file on your device to calculate dimensions, block quantities, material stacks, and a manual build order. The file is not uploaded by BlockMate as part of this local analysis.

When you explicitly enable BlockMate’s Android AccessibilityService, Android allows the app to observe the Minecraft window state and dispatch calibrated touch gestures. This access is used only for the Minecraft automation feature that you start. The service is restricted to Minecraft Bedrock’s package, `com.mojang.minecraftpe`. BlockMate does not use this access to read passwords, SMS messages, banking apps, contacts, or financial information.

BlockMate does not request or collect your Microsoft account password. It does not sell personal information. It does not use screen content for advertising, profiling, or financial activity.

## Storage and sharing

Local preferences such as onboarding completion, calibration state, and selected limits may be stored on the device. BlockMate does not sell or share this information with data brokers. The app does not upload Minecraft screen content.

If a future release adds a hosted service, this policy will be updated before that release and the Play listing will disclose the relevant data practices.

## Your choices

You can refuse to enable Accessibility access and still use the blueprint-analysis features. You can disable the service at any time from Android Settings. You can remove local app data by uninstalling BlockMate or clearing its storage.

## Children

BlockMate is not designed to collect personal information from children. It does not require an account.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app’s data practices change. The latest policy should be published at the same stable HTTPS URL shown in Google Play. For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support contact configured for the Play listing.
